About our school

Mangalore Public School and Pre-University College serves families in Mugganahalli, Kanchamaranahalli and nearby villages of Hassan district. Small classes, regular parent contact, and one campus from the early years to PU.

Sample: founded in 2008 by a charitable education trust. Karnataka State syllabus, English medium, with Kannada as a language subject.

“We want every child to leave us confident, kind and ready for what comes next.” Principal's message · sample text, name to be added

Buses run on 6 routes around Mugganahalli. Tell us your village in the enquiry and the office will confirm.

Yes. Visit any working day until 4:00 pm and meet the principal.
